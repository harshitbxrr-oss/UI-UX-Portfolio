Hi, my name is Harshit. I care about building clear, useful, and considered digital products.

I'm a UI/UX designer working across mobile and web, focused on the space where user research, interaction design, and visual craft meet. I like turning an early idea into an experience that feels calm, obvious, and intentional.

My process is hands-on: understand the problem, map the structure, design the details, and keep testing until the interface holds up under real use. I value clean systems, honest feedback, and interfaces that don't need a manual.

Outside of screens, I pay attention to the small things — type pairing, spacing rhythm, motion timing — the details that make a product feel designed rather than assembled.

How I Work

From question to interface

A four-stage process I use to keep design decisions grounded in real problems, not assumptions.

Stage 01

Discover

I start by understanding the user, the business goal, and the constraints — through interviews, competitor scans, and stakeholder conversations.

ResearchInterviewsGoals
Stage 02

Define

Findings get organized into flows, information architecture, and a clear problem statement before any screen gets drawn.

IAUser FlowsPersonas
Stage 03

Design

I move from low-fidelity wireframes to high-fidelity UI, building reusable components as I go.

WireframesUIComponents
Stage 04

Validate

Prototypes go in front of real users. I watch where they hesitate, refine the details, and hand off documented files.

TestingIterationHandoff
